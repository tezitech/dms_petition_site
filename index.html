Daggett Montessori (K-8) · 1419 College Ave, Fort Worth

Pause the contract. Plan Daggett Montessori right.

Daggett Montessori, a K-8 school, used to have a 5.9-acre campus on W Jessamine St, with its own running track. It was moved to the former De Zavala Elementary campus on College Ave, a 4-acre site built for a K-5 school. Fort Worth ISD now plans a renovation that doesn't fix that. We're asking the district to pause and plan it right, with families and teachers.

The Project

A $15M renovation of Daggett Montessori's new campus, paid for by the FWISD 2021 Bond.

From Jessamine to College Ave

Old campus

801 W Jessamine St

  • 5.9 acres of land
  • About 62,600 sq ft of buildings (footprint)
  • Running track

New campus

1419 College Ave

Previously De Zavala Elementary (K-5)

  • 4.0 acres of land
  • About 38,800 sq ft of buildings (footprint)
  • No track; built for grades K-5

The difference

  • About 1.9 acres less land (32% less)
  • About 38% less building footprint
  • About 30% less open land, before Lot A and the pond are built on it

Construction budget

$15M

Part of a roughly $29.9M bond project.

Architect

DLR Group

Managed for FWISD by PROCEDEO.

Board vote

Feb 9, 2027

Proposed Construction Phases

  1. Phase 1 (by July 31, 2027): both parking lots, the detention pond, and grading.
  2. Phase 2 (by May 31, 2028): the office addition. Classrooms move to temporary buildings during this phase.
  3. Phase 3 (done June 23, 2028): interiors and a new roof.

The Plan Doesn't Fit a K-8 Montessori

Daggett Montessori moved into a smaller campus built for De Zavala Elementary (K-5). The renovation fixes up the old building, but it doesn't make room for a K-8 Montessori.

  1. 1

    Less space

    About a third less land than our Jessamine campus, and no track.

  2. 2

    No new classrooms

    Rooms get new labels, cabinets, and sinks. No classrooms are added, and most stay the same size.

  3. 3

    A thin middle school

    One 6th-grade room, four 7th-8th grade rooms, and one science lab for grades 6-8.

  4. 4

    Montessori needs come second

    Montessori needs bigger multi-age rooms and outdoor space for gardens and outdoor learning.

  5. 5

    Parking takes the green space

    A 34-space lot and a detention pond go on the south green space. Sharing the church's lot across College Ave is an alternative.

  6. 6

    Signing locks in the money

    Once the $15M contract is signed, changes get harder and cost more.

What the $15M buys

Many of these upgrades are needed. But they fix up a K-5 building instead of planning for a K-8 Montessori.

Classrooms
  • New cabinets, sinks, and boards.
  • No new classrooms.
Science
  • One science lab for grades 6-8.
  • A small new prep room (about 260 sq ft).
Arts
  • Better auditorium sound and wheelchair seating.
  • The stage, music room, and two art rooms stay as they are.
Office & security
  • A new front office (about 2,100 sq ft) with a secure entry.
  • Card readers, a new intercom, and a 6-foot fence.
Finishes & restrooms
  • New floors, paint, and ceilings.
  • New restroom fixtures and stalls.
Accessibility
  • Lifts, rails, and accessible routes.
Building systems
  • New heating and cooling, lighting, sprinklers, and fire alarm.
  • New windows, keeping the historic look on the front.
Site & parking
  • Lot A: 34 spaces on the south green space.
  • A detention pond (about 5,400 sq ft) for runoff from the new paving.
  • Lot B: 20 spaces, rebuilt across Alston Ave.

What We're Asking For

  1. Pause the contract. Delay the Feb 9, 2027 board vote until there's a real plan.
  2. Plan for a K-8 Montessori first. Set the project's requirements with the PTA, teachers, school leaders, Montessori experts, and neighbors: classrooms, middle school, science, outdoor learning, and parking.
  3. Build from that plan. Revise the design and the contractor proposal to match it.
  4. Solve parking without paving green space. Look at sharing the church's lot and keeping some Alston parking.

We're not asking FWISD to cancel this investment, or to drop the parking lot and build the rest as drawn. We want the whole project planned around Daggett Montessori before the money is spent.
